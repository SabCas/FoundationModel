# KUBECA Market Image Prompts

These prompts are for the three image tiles in `04 / WHERE KUBECA CREATES VALUE`.

## Shared Art Direction

- Cinematic, high-trust aerospace photography.
- Realistic and restrained, not science fiction or military game art.
- Natural desaturated color: cool stone, muted green, concrete gray, soft sky; retain subtle color rather than black and white.
- Documentary perspective with clear operational context and one visible small aerial drone.
- No weapons, explosions, fire, combat action, logos, UI overlays, maps, text, icons, borders, or orange/gold color grading.
- No dramatic drone swarm; keep the system credible and human-supervised.
- Vertical composition suitable for a tall editorial tile, `4:5` aspect ratio.
- Preserve clear subject separation and sufficient negative space; the HTML provides all labels and copy.

## 01 / Defense / Tactical ISR

Purpose: initial focus and most credible first market.

Prompt:

```text
Photorealistic editorial aerospace image, vertical 4:5. A small fixed-wing or compact VTOL reconnaissance drone flying above a remote mountainous valley at early morning, observed from a safe standoff position by one field operator in the lower foreground with a compact rugged tablet or ground-control case. Wide terrain visibility, distant ridgelines, difficult access, clear sense of range and surveillance without combat. Natural subdued color, slate mountains, muted olive clothing, cool overcast light with soft warm daylight on the horizon. Serious European defence-technology visual language, realistic equipment, cinematic but understated, sharp details, no weapons, no explosions, no logos, no text, no HUD graphics.
```

## 02 / Disaster Response / Search Operations

Purpose: adjacent application, showing safe access to damaged areas.

Prompt:

```text
Photorealistic editorial rescue-operations image, vertical 4:5. A compact mapping drone flying slowly above a damaged urban or industrial structure after a natural disaster, with two professional rescue responders at a safe distance reviewing information on a rugged tablet. Rubble, unstable concrete, blocked access route, no injured people visible and no active danger. The drone is gathering local information where human entry is unsafe. Natural desaturated color, concrete gray, muted safety clothing, cloudy daylight, documentary realism, calm and professional atmosphere, no fire, no explosions, no logos, no text, no interface overlay.
```

## 03 / Critical Infrastructure Security

Purpose: future platform expansion into complex monitored sites.

Prompt:

Photorealistic editorial infrastructure-security image, vertical 4:5. A small autonomous inspection drone flying above a large energy or industrial facility with storage tanks, service roads, pipelines, and perimeter structures visible in an orderly wide composition. One distant safety or operations specialist observes from a controlled area, emphasizing supervised inspection rather than threat response. Natural desaturated color with muted steel, concrete, pale green and blue-gray sky, clean daylight, sophisticated industrial realism, high-trust technology aesthetic, no emergency, no weapons, no logos, no text, no HUD graphics.

## Consistency Check

Generate the three images as one visual family:

- Same level of contrast and saturation.
- Same camera realism and subdued daylight treatment.
- Drone scale remains modest and plausible.
- Humans appear as supervising professionals, not action-film subjects.
- Each image reads clearly when cropped into the market tile media area.
