# KUBECA Investor Roadmap Memo

## Purpose

This memo expands the website roadmap into a diligence-ready view of what KUBECA intends to build, test, finance, and de-risk across the first three company phases.

The website should make the plan understandable quickly. This memo is the deeper investor material for technical and financing conversations.

## Roadmap Thesis

KUBECA first proves the carrier-enabled deployment system, then upgrades local drones into intelligent supervised agents, and finally scales the architecture into a regulated multi-agent mission-intelligence platform.

## Phase Summary

| Round | Phase | Main Proof Point | Main Risk Reduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Seed / Integrated Prototype | Phase 1 | Carrier releases GNSS-denied quadcopters for supervised local operation | Technical integration risk |
| Seed | Phase 2 | Local drones gain mapping, spatial understanding, mission-state reporting, and early coordination | Autonomy / software risk |
| Series A | Phase 3 | Multi-agent supervised exploration, persistent observation, and regulated operational readiness | Scale / certification / platform risk |

## Phase 1 - Pre-Seed / Integrated Prototype

### Carrier-Enabled GNSS-Denied Deployment

Goal: validate the first concrete aerial configuration: a GNSS-resilient fixed-wing carrier that transports and releases GNSS-denied quadcopters for local supervised operation.

### What Is Built

- Fixed-wing carrier platform.
- Payload integration for small quadcopters.
- Release mechanism.
- GNSS-resilient carrier navigation.
- GNSS-denied quadcopter operation.
- Basic operator workflow.
- Basic telemetry / mission-state view.
- Early relay architecture preparation.
- Test and safety documentation.

### What Phase 1 Proves

- The carrier can fly with payload.
- The carrier can transport and release quadcopters.
- The release mechanism works reliably.
- The quadcopters can operate locally without GNSS.
- The operator can supervise the carrier-to-quadcopter handover.
- The system creates value before full multi-agent autonomy.

### Risk Reduced

Technical integration risk.

### Investor Sentence

Phase 1 proves the physical system: carrier reach, reliable deployment, GNSS-denied local operation, and supervised handover.

## Phase 2 - Seed

### Local Drone Intelligence And Early Coordination

Goal: upgrade released quadcopters into supervised local drone agents with mapping, localization, spatial understanding, mission-state reporting, and early coordination.

### What Is Built

- Local mapping.
- Visual-inertial localization.
- Spatial understanding.
- Scene / object awareness.
- Mission-state reporting.
- Basic task execution.
- Mission software v1.
- Stronger operator interface.
- Early multi-drone coordination.
- Early carrier/local-drone coordination.
- Improved relay / communication layer.
- Certification and safety preparation.

### Prior Evidence Context

Prior SPRIND/AVIANS-related work supports this phase because it included stereo-visual-inertial SLAM, metric mapping and state estimation, object detection/tracking, scene-graph generation, semantic-aware planning, mission execution, and planned multi-drone semantic mapping.

### What Phase 2 Proves

- Local drones are not only FPV assets.
- They can become supervised autonomous agents.
- They can map and understand spatial context.
- They can report mission state to the system.
- The mission software can coordinate carrier and local assets.
- Early multi-agent behavior can be tested under human supervision.
- The company begins building certification-relevant documentation.

### Risk Reduced

Autonomy and software risk.

Secondary risk reduced: validation and regulatory preparation risk.

### Investor Sentence

Phase 2 proves the intelligence layer: mapping, spatial understanding, mission-state reporting, and early supervised coordination.

## Phase 3 - Series A

### Regulated Supervised Autonomous Mission Operations

Goal: scale KUBECA from a validated aerial system into a supervised multi-agent mission platform for autonomous exploration, persistent observation, cooperative mapping, and coordinated mission operations.

### What Is Built

- Stronger multi-agent coordination.
- Cooperative mapping.
- Indoor and outdoor autonomous exploration.
- Persistent observation.
- Shared mission picture.
- Task allocation across assets.
- Multiple carrier/local-drone configurations.
- Partner evaluation units.
- Pilot-production preparation.
- Safety case development.
- Certification and operational approval pathway.
- Future integration of ground robots, additional sensors, or maritime nodes.

### What Phase 3 Proves

- The system can scale beyond one carrier and one local drone.
- Multiple assets can share mission context.
- Operators can supervise coordinated autonomy.
- KUBECA can move toward regulated operational readiness.
- The mission-intelligence layer becomes the platform.
- The system can expand toward future cross-domain assets.

### Risk Reduced

Scale, certification, and platform risk.

### Investor Sentence

Phase 3 proves the platform: supervised multi-agent mission operations, persistent observation, autonomous exploration, and regulated operational readiness.

## Certification And Validation Pathway

KUBECA should treat certification as a staged pathway, not a completed status. Validation should build from controlled ground tests and flight tests toward partner evaluation readiness, safety documentation, repeatable system evidence, and later regulated operational approval preparation.
